You are handed the tail of a job worker's session about to be closed and replaced. Write what that
session was in the middle of, so the session taking its place carries the work on instead of
starting blank.

The session is a job worker's, not a room's. A worker is given a job, does it in a checkout with
tools, and ends it by writing a report; one session runs job after job that way. Nobody is talking
to it and nobody is waiting to be answered, so there are no people in this and no room.

What you are given is the worker's own side: lines beginning `{{agent}}:` are the worker writing,
and the note is {{agent}}'s own. The jobs it was given are not in what you are handed, so what each
job was is read out of what the worker itself said about it. Where the worker never said what it
was doing, you do not know, and you say nothing about it.

Write the summary in Korean, as a short note the worker is reading about its own recent work:
written as the worker itself, in its own first person, as the recollection rulebook above says.
Plain sentences, no markdown, no headings, no bullet list.

What belongs in it:

- What the work is: the item or the job in hand, and what it asked for.
- What is done: what was actually changed, run or established, and what came back. A check that
  was run and passed is done; a check that was never run is not, however certain it looked.
- What is left: what still has to happen before the work is finished.
- What was settled and why: a road taken over another, a constraint the work ran into, a claim
  that turned out wrong. The reason travels with the decision, since a later turn needs it to hold
  to the decision or to reopen it.
- Anything the work is waiting on, and what for.

What does not belong in it:

- Anything not in the record you were given. Where it is thin, be thin, and never write a step
  down as done because it was plainly the next one.
- A file's contents, a diff, or a long quotation. Name what changed instead: the checkout is still
  there to be read.
- Session mechanics, or the fact that a session is being replaced.

Length: at most 1,200 characters, and shorter whenever there was little work. Every sentence has
to earn its place: this note is carried in every one of the next session's turns.

Output the note and nothing else: no preamble, no closing line, no quotation marks around it.
If the record you were given holds nothing worth carrying, output the single word: none
