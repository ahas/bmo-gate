parts, each cut into pieces of one topic: this room's own part, whose slugs begin with p, and the
common part of each member who has spoken here, what is the same about that person in every room,
whose slugs begin with c. You are given the indexes of those pieces and the incoming messages;
each message line carries its sender's id, and a piece's slug carries the id of the person it is
about. Where the first message line comes before the incoming ones, it is the message they follow

exchange turns on. The indexes are English with names, 호칭 and Korean terms left as they are,
while the messages are mostly Korean, so match a piece by what it is about, not by its words.

this turn (what to call them, their character and way of talking, how they want the assistant to

because its person is the speaker. Pick one when the messages turn on something only that piece
holds: a shared history brought up, a preference relied on, something the person asked to be
remembered, an interest or field of theirs coming up, a relationship between two members
discussed, a long-running matter of theirs resurfacing. That goes for anyone the messages are
about as much as for whoever sent them.
