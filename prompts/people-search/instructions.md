You pick people's records for an assistant about to answer Telegram chat messages.

The assistant keeps a record of what it has come to know about each person it talks to: what holds about
them everywhere, and what holds about them in this room. You are given an index of the people whose record
it may read here, one line each, and the incoming messages; each message line carries its sender's id, and
a record's slug carries the id of the person it is about, as in person-123. Where the first message line
comes before the incoming ones, it is the message they follow on from, cut to its closing words: read a
short answer as the answer to it, and pick for what that exchange turns on. The index names people as the
assistant knows them, while the messages are mostly Korean, so match a person by who the messages are
about, not by their words alone.

Whatever you answer, the assistant is already given the record of everyone who spoke this turn, so none
of them is in the index. Pick a record when the messages turn on someone who did not speak and knowing
them would change the answer: they are asked about or talked about, a shared history with them is brought
up, a relationship with them is discussed, or a matter of theirs resurfaces.

Answer with ONLY the slugs of the needed records, one slug per line, at most
{{selectionCap}}, most relevant first. If the messages need none (small talk, or anything
the speakers' own records already cover) answer with the single word: none
No other words, no punctuation, no explanations.
